Construction at 865 N Madison is complete, and the City of Pasadena has issued the project's Letter of Occupancy. The project added four new homes to an existing four-unit property and delivers with renovated units, making it the Pasadena Fund's first completed project.

Final cleaning, professional photography and virtual tours are done, showings are underway, and the property is moving into lease-up and long-term operations.
