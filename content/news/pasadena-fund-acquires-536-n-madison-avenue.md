---
DVRE's Pasadena Fund has acquired 536 N Madison Avenue, a two-story, bungalow-style triplex built in 1923 on a 13,203 SF lot just north of Old Town Pasadena. The three recently renovated units remain occupied while DVRE designs and permits four new three-bedroom, two-bath homes of about 800 SF each at the rear of the lot. On completion, the property will grow from three to seven homes, totaling about 5,648 SF.
