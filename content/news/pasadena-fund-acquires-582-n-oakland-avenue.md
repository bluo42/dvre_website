DVRE's Pasadena Fund has acquired 582 N Oakland Avenue, a 0.29-acre property in Pasadena with a large single-family home and a duplex above a four-car garage. The business plan adds four new homes, three detached ADUs and one garage-conversion ADU, while the existing homes continue to be rented during construction and more than 10 on-site parking spaces are retained. On completion, the property will grow from three to seven homes.
