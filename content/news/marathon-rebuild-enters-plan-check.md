title: Marathon rebuild enters plan check
date: 2026-09-29
category: Construction
fund: Altadena Fund
excerpt: Two adjacent Altadena lots are being rebuilt as eight build-to-rent homes.
---
Plans for DVRE's Marathon project, two adjacent lots in Altadena affected by the Eaton Fire, have been submitted for plan check. Each lot is planned for four new homes under California's SB 9 lot-split provisions, adding eight code-compliant rental homes to the neighborhood.
