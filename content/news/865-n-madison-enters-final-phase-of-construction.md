category: Construction

Construction at 865 N Madison has reached its final phase. Interior trades, including water heaters, plumbing, electrical and cabinetry, are wrapping up ahead of the power release inspection. Two units vacated during the project are being remodeled so the property delivers fully renovated, and the project remains on budget, with completion targeted for summer 2026.
