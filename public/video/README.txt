Squarespace streams video in a format that can't be embedded elsewhere, so download
your original drone clip (or export it from Squarespace: Asset Library → the video →
Download) and save it here as hero.mp4. Keep it under ~15 MB (1080p, 20–30 s, no audio)
so it loads fast. Until it's added, the homepage shows the still photo instead.
